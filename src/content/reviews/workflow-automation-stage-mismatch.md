This review is about us, not them. SmarterOutbound did their job well. The campaigns produced meetings, the deliverability was strong, the copy was on-point. Where I'd push back is that we hired them too early.

At ~$30k MRR, the engagement fee was a meaningful chunk of our runway. The meetings they booked were good, but at our stage I could have produced 60-70% of the same outcome by running cold email myself with a $99/month tool and 8 hours per week.

The remaining 30-40% productivity gain wasn't worth the burn for us at that revenue level. By Series A it would have been worth it. We jumped early.

## Four stars

The work was five-star. The decision to hire was a stage mismatch on our side. If we hire them again post-Series A, this would be a five-star review.

If you're an early-stage founder reading this: do the math on stage. The engagement is great if you can afford it. If you can't, do it yourself first and graduate to outsourced later.
