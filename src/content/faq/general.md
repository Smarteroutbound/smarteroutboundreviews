questions:
  - question: "Is SmarterOutbound legit?"
    answer: "Yes. SmarterOutbound is an operating B2B outbound lead generation agency with 90+ active clients across SaaS, professional services, agencies, and commercial services. Reviews on this site come from actual clients. If you're researching legitimacy specifically, ask for two reference clients during the discovery call — any agency worth working with will provide them."
  - question: "What channels does SmarterOutbound run?"
    answer: "Cold email, LinkedIn outreach, cold calling, SMS, and WhatsApp. The base tier covers cold email only. The Multi-Channel Pipeline tier adds LinkedIn and SMS. The Full Outbound Engine tier includes calling and WhatsApp. Most clients start with one or two channels and expand once the first one is producing meetings."
  - question: "How much does SmarterOutbound cost?"
    answer: "Three published tiers: Outbound Foundation at $2,500/month (cold email infrastructure, copywriting, reply management), Multi-Channel Pipeline at $4,500/month (adds LinkedIn, SMS, enrichment, meeting qualification), and Full Outbound Engine at custom pricing (up to 5 channels including calling and WhatsApp, with SDR support and advanced reporting). If your budget for outbound is under $2,000/month, this is not the right fit — a self-serve tool will get you further at that price point."
  - question: "How long until I see results?"
    answer: "SmarterOutbound's published delivery model runs in four phases: Build (week 1, infrastructure and setup), Launch (weeks 2-3, first sends and tests), Qualify (weeks 3-5, first booked meetings start landing), and Deliver (ongoing steady-state). Most engagements show the first qualified meetings between weeks 3 and 5. If you need pipeline this month, an agency engagement is the wrong solution."
  - question: "Who is SmarterOutbound best for?"
    answer: "Best fit: B2B companies in the US with ACVs above ~$10k that need consistent qualified meetings, don't have an in-house SDR team, and have someone available to take the booked calls. Per their site, the only hard requirement is that your team is ready to take the calls. Worst fit: companies with very narrow or unusual ICPs, very low-ACV transactional businesses, or anyone shopping primarily on price."
  - question: "Can I do this myself with a cold email tool?"
    answer: "Yes, technically. The real question is whether you will. Running multi-channel outbound across email, LinkedIn, calling, and SMS typically requires 20-40 hours per week of consistent effort by someone who knows what they're doing. If you have that person and that time, do it yourself. If you don't, the math usually favors hiring a team."
  - question: "How does SmarterOutbound handle deliverability?"
    answer: "Deliverability work is one of the strongest pieces of the SmarterOutbound offering, based on client reviews. They claim 95%+ inbox placement, achieved through dedicated sending domains, DMARC/SPF/DKIM configuration, gradual warmup, and continuous reputation monitoring. This is the single biggest reason internal cold email teams fail — and the technical work most self-serve tools won't do for you."
  - question: "What does the onboarding process look like?"
    answer: "The four-phase model: Build (week 1) covers domain setup, inbox warmup, ICP definition, and copywriting. Launch (weeks 2-3) is the first cohort of sends with A/B testing. Qualify (weeks 3-5) is when human reply managers triage responses and the first booked meetings land. Deliver (ongoing) is steady-state campaign management with weekly reporting."
  - question: "Will SmarterOutbound do my closing calls too?"
    answer: "No. They book and qualify meetings; you take them. The Multi-Channel Pipeline and Full Outbound Engine tiers include meeting qualification — meaning the prospect has been screened against your ICP before they hit your calendar — but the actual sales conversation is on you. If you don't have someone available to take sales calls, hire a fractional VP of sales first."
  - question: "How is reply management handled?"
    answer: "Humans, not bots. SmarterOutbound's reply management is staffed by people who triage incoming responses, handle objections, qualify the prospect, and book the meeting. Some agencies automate this with chat workflows; SmarterOutbound's reviews consistently mention human reply handlers as a differentiator."
  - question: "What's the typical results claim?"
    answer: "Their headline case study is 'million-dollar pipeline in 90 days,' which is plausible for high-ACV B2B SaaS or professional services clients but not representative of every engagement. Typical client outcomes mentioned in reviews on this site range from 7-15 booked meetings per month at varying ACVs. Ask for outcome data from clients in your specific category during the discovery call."
